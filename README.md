# Starwarden

Play it here: https://videobyisaac.github.io/starwarden/

You are the Warden of a frontier space station. Petitioners come to you one at a time: swipe right for yes, left for no, and keep the Navy, the Docks, the Faithful and the Treasury all in balance. Let any of them fall to nothing or grow too strong, and your tenure is over.

This repository holds only the built web version of the game.
